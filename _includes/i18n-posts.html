{%- comment -%} Sets the post lists for `lang` (set by i18n.html): i18n_posts published posts written in `lang`, newest first i18n_listing i18n_posts plus, for a translated language, every post of the default language that has no translation into `lang`; for the home page and tag index, so no post is hidden from readers who were redirected to their language Jekyll keeps future-dated documents in custom collections (it only skips writing their pages), so they are filtered out here unless the build runs with --future. {%- endcomment -%} {%- if lang == site.languages.first -%} {%- assign i18n_posts = site.posts -%} {%- assign i18n_listing = site.posts -%} {%- else -%} {%- assign i18n_coll = "posts_" | append: lang -%} {%- assign i18n_posts = site[i18n_coll] -%} {%- unless site.future -%} {%- assign i18n_posts = i18n_posts | where_exp: "post", "post.date <= site.time" -%} {%- endunless -%} {%- assign i18n_posts = i18n_posts | reverse -%} {%- assign i18n_refs = i18n_posts | map: "ref" | compact -%} {%- assign i18n_listing = i18n_posts -%} {%- for post in site.posts -%} {%- unless post.ref and i18n_refs contains post.ref -%} {%- assign i18n_listing = i18n_listing | push: post -%} {%- endunless -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- assign i18n_listing = i18n_listing | sort: "date" | reverse -%} {%- endif -%}
