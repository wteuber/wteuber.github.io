The tax staircase is a Bresenham line

A 36-atom bill, paid in equal installments. Laid out atom by atom along the bottom, the tax owed rises as a straight line to the right. The floored running total lights one whole pixel per atom, kept as close to that line as the grid allows — blue where the atom is net, orange where it is tax. The orange pixels land by the same rule a screen uses to draw a slanted line. (The vertical axis is stretched so a shallow real-world rate stays legible.)
