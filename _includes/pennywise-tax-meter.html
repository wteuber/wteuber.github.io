Tax meter

A tax-inclusive bill, paid in equal installments. Each installment is split two ways: the everyday accounting-software approach of rounding its tax on its own, and PennyWise’s running floor. Watch the totals reconcile, or not.

Standard method’s running tax error — above the line it has collected too much, below, too little

“Standard rounding” here is each installment’s tax taken on its own as round(installment × rate ÷ (1 + rate)), then netted — exactly what a receipt line shows. Across a plan the pieces rarely add back to the invoice. PennyWise reads one floored running total instead, so the installments always sum to the bill and no receipt is ever off by more than a cent.
