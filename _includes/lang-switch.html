{%- comment -%} Language pill next to the night mode button (styles in assets/css/lang-switch.css), built to look and behave like it: the pill shows the flag of the page's language with a knob carrying a thin line globe (white knob, or dark in the dark theme) as the slider on the left, where the other pill has its sun or moon. Hovering previews the other language (its flag fades in and the globe slides right); clicking keeps that state and opens the translation. Included twice by nav.html: next to the night mode button, and in the collapsed menu for narrow screens (the CSS shows one of them). Links to `ls_alt`, which nav.html looks up from `i18n_alts` (i18n-alternates.html); renders nothing when the page has no translation. {%- endcomment -%} {%- if ls_alt -%} {%- unless lang_switch_script -%}{%- assign lang_switch_script = true %} {%- endunless -%} {%- endif -%}
