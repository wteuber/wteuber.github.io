Both of my kids had an idea for a game of their own, and they knew exactly how it should work. So we sat down together. Each of them told me all about their game and drew a rough sketch of it on paper. Then I used [Claude Code](https://claude.com/claude-code) with the Fable 5.2 model to help them turn the idea and the sketch into a real game that runs in the browser.

The best part wasn't the code. It was the talking: listening to them explain how their game works, asking questions, and figuring out the details together. And then, of course, playing. We had so much fun!

Here are their two games. Drag the handle across each picture to see how close the game stayed to the sketch it started from.

## Dupliziere die Nummern

Find two circles with the same number and tap them, but stay away from the black stones wandering around the page. The game is in German, just like its designer.

## Scribble Slide
